Before Programming Your Radio

Programming typically takes less than one minute, based on our physical testing. Programming time is generally similar regardless of the number of channels in use.

I have checked and confirmed the following:

  • Bluetooth distance: My radio is within 3–6 feet (1–2 meters) of my computer, with minimal obstructions.
  • Radio battery: My radio has sufficient battery power to complete programming without interruption.
  • Computer power: My computer is plugged in or sufficiently charged, and sleep mode will not interrupt programming.
  • Browser: I will not close, refresh, or navigate away from this website while programming is underway.
  • Radio operation: I will not turn off or operate the radio until programming is complete.
  • Backup: I have an existing CPS .dat backup and accept the risk of radio damage.
Experimental Software — Radio Damage Risk

Programming errors, software defects, or interrupted communication may render your radio inoperable. Always maintain a backup dat for use with official CPS and programming cable.

Reconnect after radio restart

Wait until the radio has finished restarting and Wireless CPS is ON. Stop scanning and close other programming apps. Reconnect when ready; if it fails, toggle Wireless CPS OFF, then ON and use the fallback button.
